Reconnect to Your Body, Reconnect to Your True Self

When we are under stress, we can lose contact with our bodies. We become caught up in our thoughts and move into survival mode. Instead of really being present in the here and now, we may simply function, feeling disconnected, overwhelmed, lost or without meaning.

Through Yoga, Qi Gong and Meditation, I guide you towards reconnecting with yourself by calming your nervous system, releasing tension in your body and quieting your “monkey mind.”

Come and explore how to shift your attention from the outside world to your inner experience, and discover the stillness that has always been there, all around you, even in the midst of chaos.

Sometimes, it is as simple as shifting your attention to change your perception, and suddenly the outside world becomes quiet, even though the chaos around you remains the same. When you relax, you can perceive things differently.
