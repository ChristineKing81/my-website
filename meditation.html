Meditation

Yoga and Qi Gong can be a beautiful entrance into meditation. As the body becomes more relaxed and settled, it can become easier to sit still, slow down and turn your attention inward.

Meditation begins with something very simple:

Can I actually be here, in this moment?

So much of our time is spent somewhere else — thinking about what happened, worrying about what might happen, wishing things were different or resisting what is happening now.

Yet this moment is the only place where we can actually experience our lives.

Meditation gives us the opportunity to become curious about what is happening inside: our thoughts, emotions, sensations, reactions and beliefs. Rather than immediately becoming absorbed in them, we learn to observe them.

This gradually creates awareness, and awareness gives us choice. Instead of automatically reacting to every thought, feeling or situation, we can begin to notice what is happening and choose how to respond.

Meditation has been used across many cultures to explore fundamental questions such as: Who am I? What is truly important? What makes a fulfilling life?
